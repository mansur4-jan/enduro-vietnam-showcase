TOUR OVERVIEW

A short but rich 4-day tour covering North Saigon, central highlands, and coastal roads, including Cat Tien National Park and Mui Ne.

Itinerary

Day 1. Saigon - Nam Cat Tien National Park (180km) Start this amazing 4 day motorbike tour south Vietnam with the ride off the beaten track to Cat Tien National park!Pick you up at the hotel at around 8:30 am, take you to our starting point and start the ride at around 9 am after rush hour. Just a short ride we will be on the bank of Dong Nai river where we take a ferry to the other side where our ride is more interesting to Tri An lake. This lake is one of the largest lakes in Vietnam and built for producing hydropower.After lunch, the ride will be more enjoyable, We will take the back roads through the jungle of Ma Da. Here we have an opportunity to explore the tunnels of Duong Minh Chau base of the Viet Cong in the 60s. Next, you continue your tour on smaller roads through the fertile natural reserve before you get on another ferry taking us to nearby Nam Cat Tien National Park. This park is a biodiversity park hosting many rare mammals, birds, reptiles, and flora. Overnight in a lodge next to the park. Day 2. Nam Cat Tien - Ta Dung Lake (160km) Today is even greater with some fun offroad motorbike ride in central highlands and the view is more beautiful. After breakfast, get on the bike and hit the road to Da Teh, an isolated town surrounded by tea and coffee plantations, and continue over mountains passes. Let yourself get swallowed up in the picturesque scenic highland, a sea of green color everywhere you look to the endless horizon.We will arrive the lake in the early afternoon, check in the room and have time to relax to walk for some lake view or we can motorbike more around to discover some villages surroundings Day 3. Ta Dung Lake - Mui Ne (190km) After breakfast, we take a stunning winding motorbike ride down to the coast to the oceanfront town of Mui Ne. The trip certainly can stop at vegetable and flower farms, coffee plantations to get a look at the changes in land use as we emerge from the lower central highlands. We can do some fun offorad motorbike ride in the morning. The road hits 1000 meters as we first ascend before traversing the pass for the descent. This south Vietnam motorbike tour also includes some spectacular views along the way down to the coast.We will stop in Mui Ne for a relaxing afternoon on the beach and taking in some of the nightlife of busy Mui Ne. Day 4. Mui Ne - Saigon (250km) Today is a big and great day with a lot of things to do and see. It’s a pity if you miss the chances to explore the simple life of locals with their fishing net, small wooden boats in the early morning. It is usually to see the one-man traditional fishing vessel, the coracle.We will start the tour back to Saigon with amazing ride on the breath-taking coastal roads crossing some salt farms and many dragon fruit plantations. This motorcycle ride will get closer to the sea in Ho Tram and Ba Ria then the last part is the slowly ride on the busy road to the city of Saigon. If you can’t enjoy the ride with the local traffic, we can drop the bikes out of the city, saying good bye to the guide team ofVietnam Motorbike Tour Expertand we will take the taxi back to your hotel in city center of Saigon.
