TOUR OVERVIEW

A 5-day escape from the hustle of Ho Chi Minh City to Nha Trang, featuring coastal rides and fun off-road sections in the central highlands.

Itinerary

Day 1. Saigon - Cu Chi tunnels - Dong Xoai (150km) Start this great motorbike tour from Saigon at 9 AM from your hotel in Ho Chi Minh city. We will be at Cu Chi tunnel after around 2 hour motorbike ride through some villages in the countryside to the North of Saigon, spend around 2 hours to discover the tunnel and learn more about the history of Vietnam War. After lunch, we keep riding through some large rubber tree garden to Dong Xoai town where the Ho Chi Minh trail end in the south of Vietnam. It could be a fun offroad motorbike tour crossing some rubber tree forest before arriving in a little quiet town of Dong XoaiDinner and overnight in Hotel in Dong Xoai. Day 2. Dong Xoai - Dak Mil (195km) After breakfast, we continue the Vietnam motorbike tour on the Ho Chi Minh trail and do take some visits of beautiful villages, War monument and cashew nut factory especially the trail takes us along the Cambodia border. Today is long ride and it must be the best of this South Vietnam motorbike tour from Saigon because it offers nice view of mountains in the highlands beside plenty of visits to the historical sites and ethnic people that make you understand better about the Vietnamese history and culture.Overnight in Dak Mil Day 3. Dak Mil - Lak Lake (110km) Keep motorcycle touring on Ho Chi Minh trail and stop more at the villages of hill tribe (M’nong, Ba Na people). We will pass some beautiful waterfall with a green water pool where we can have great swim. After picnic lunch at one of the waterfall, we motorcycle to the Lak lake – the second largest lake in Vietnam and spend the rest of the day at the lake (swimming or hunting the pictures of sunset).Overnight in a guesthouse nearby the lake – Home stay. Day 4. Lak Lake - Da lat (180km) This motorbike tour to Da Lat must be another highlight of this 5 day motorbike touring from Saigon. It’s quite a long ride on and offroad but we will have a lot of chances to visit mushroom farm or flower garden, waterfall… short trek up hills or villages of the hill tribes. When we get close to Da Lat, we can have an enjoyable offroad motorcycle tour through the pine tree hills to Da Lat.Overnight in a hotel in Da Lat. Day 5. Da Lat - Nha Trang (150km) This is a easy motorbike tour to Nha Trang, most of the ride is on big and smooth road except the small “monkey bridge” just after Da Lat town. We will do some stops to visit King Bao Dai’s palace or peaceful villages of the hill tribe along the way.The 5 Day South Vietnam motorbike tour from Saigon to Nha Trang will finish at around 4.30 PM in Nha Trang city after saying good bye to the staff ofVietnam Motorbike Tour Expertyou can enjoy your free relaxing time at one of the most beautiful beach in the world before flying back home or back to Ho Chi Minh city.
