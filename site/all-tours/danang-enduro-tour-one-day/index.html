TOUR OVERVIEW

This enduro tour is your ticket to the breathtaking landscapes of Da Nang, where the mountains literally meet the sea. Forget the city traffic and head to the Son Tra Peninsula. You'll ride through dense tropical forests on red clay trails, tackle rocky climbs, and reach heights where the entire Danang Bay is visible. The route includes abandoned military roads and narrow paths used only by locals. You will experience the contrast between the salty sea breeze and the humid mountain air. This is a concentrated dose of adrenaline, technical riding, and stunning coastal scenery. Perfect for those who want to see the wild side of Da Nang in just a few hours!

ABOUT TOUR

1. Collection and preparation

Introduction and equipment We meet you in the cafe at the bottom of our house, get to know each other and go up to the apartment. While we pick up the equipment and dress, we discuss your experience (where and how often you have ridden). Based on this, we select the difficulty of the route: we train beginners on the lai, experienced riders are immediately taken to the average difficulty.

2. Instruction: Technique and Traffic Regulations

Includes: Transfer to the starting point, detailed instructions, and a trial run. Here, you learn how to operate the equipment under the supervision of the guides and take your first confident steps (or runs) before entering the main track.

3. Arrival: escort and content

We set a time limit and set out on the route. The guide does not stray more than 150 meters, waiting for all participants in the shade or on flat areas. During the tour, we provide advice, encourage participants at difficult stages, and take photos/videos at all viewing points.

4. Completion: inspection

Includes: A snack on the route and a return to the base. The final stage, where you can rest, share your impressions over a snack, and return to the collection point in comfort.
